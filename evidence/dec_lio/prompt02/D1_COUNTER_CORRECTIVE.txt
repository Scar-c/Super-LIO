  Each thread-local accumulator increments `used_residual_count` immediately
  after both native H/b accumulation statements. Integer counters are summed

  analyzer receives `candidate_count=effect_knn_num_` and the independent
  `used_residual_count`; no native H, b, residual, Jacobian, weight, map, or
  estimator update is changed.
