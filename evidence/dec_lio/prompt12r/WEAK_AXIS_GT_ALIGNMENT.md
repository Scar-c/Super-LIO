# Weak-axis GT alignment

GT frame classification is GT-B, not GT-A. Consequently:

```text
authorized GT weak-axis projection: NO
1 s f_weak: not claimed as GT-aligned
5 s f_weak: not claimed as GT-aligned
high-cond vs low-cond GT weak-axis effect: not authorized
```

The valid scalar SO(3) APE/RPE values are reported in
`FULL6DOF_COMPARISON.md`. The N-reference projection is reported separately
and does not upgrade GT-B to GT-A.
