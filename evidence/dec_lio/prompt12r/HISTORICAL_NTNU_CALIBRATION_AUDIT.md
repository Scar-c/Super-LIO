# Historical NTNU calibration audit

The historical `ntnu-arl/lidar_degeneracy_datasets` repository reports an
OS0-128/VN100 calibration approximately:

```text
[-0.00171, 0.02149, 0.0358,
  0.000462, 0.0008483, 0.0028835, 0.9999954]
```

This is platform-lineage corroboration only. It does not prove the same
physical rig or acquisition-specific calibration as either released NTNU
bag; it is classified HIST-B and is not averaged with E1 or used to choose
the final transform.

Source: https://github.com/ntnu-arl/lidar_degeneracy_datasets
