# Prompt20 classification clarification

Prompt20's final report presented P20-A, P20-B, P20-C, and P20-D as if they
were simultaneous final categories. Under the original Prompt20 classification
contract, the categories are mutually exclusive. The corrected overall
classification is:

```text
P20-A — SUBSPACE_LOOSE_SUPPORTED
```

This records that the pose-level loose-fusion architecture was supported under
the Prompt20 state-health gates. Directional causality was not identified by
Prompt20 alone: L1 changed both total covariance and directional structure.
That causal question remains open until the Prompt21 trace-matched L2 control.

Historical Prompt20 files are not rewritten.
