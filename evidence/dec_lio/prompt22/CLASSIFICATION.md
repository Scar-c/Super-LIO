# Prompt22 classification

## P22-B — INFORMATION_DIRECTIONALITY_SEQUENCE_DEPENDENT

Information parity, diagnostic identity, and state-health gates pass. The
controlled L1/L3 comparison is not uniform:

```text
Stairs Alpha:       L3 0.356768 m < L1 0.474189 m
Tunnel2 Alpha:      L1 2.519871 m < L3 2.528457 m  (marginal)
Tunnel2 Gamma:      L1 1.414167 m < L3 2.080833 m
```

L3 therefore wins the key Stairs comparison, while L1 wins the two tunnel
sensor cases. This rules out P22-A as a universal directional benefit and
rules out P22-C/D. The result is not P22-E because information parity,
existing-mode identity, covariance validity, and evaluator contracts all pass.

The evidence motivates a future prior-vs-LiDAR authority model, but Prompt22
does not implement one.
