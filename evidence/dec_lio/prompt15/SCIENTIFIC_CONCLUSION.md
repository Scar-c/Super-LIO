# Prompt15 scientific conclusion

Prompt15 does not assign a fraction of final ATE to historical frames. The
estimator is nonlinear and recursive: intervention changes future state,
deskew, map insertion, correspondences, Jacobians, and later corrections, so
per-frame additive attribution is ill-defined.

Among six events with sufficient long-horizon GT coverage, two showed
persistent downstream harm, two recovered toward the native branch, and two
were mixed. No event showed persistent downstream benefit. Three additional
tunnel1 events are unavailable because the supplied GT starts after the event.

Therefore the result is not evidence that releasing every suppressed LiDAR
correction is beneficial, nor evidence that all suppression is protective.
It is mixed counterfactual sensitivity under an intentionally inconsistent
mean-only covariance probe. Standard APE/RPE/segment errors remain trajectory
metrics; this replay is an internal causal diagnostic, consistent with the
trajectory-level evaluation convention used by X-ICP, BIEVR-LIO, and DCReg.
