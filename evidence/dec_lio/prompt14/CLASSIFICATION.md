# Prompt14 classification

## Classification: P14-B

The audit is classified as **P14-B — sparse/mixed prior-suppression
phenomenon; proceed only as a shadow/prototype**.

Basis for the classification:

- all five required GEODE sequences completed with valid inputs;
- native and shadow trajectories are byte-identical;
- all frames satisfy the basis and correspondence contract in the v2 CSVs;
- weak-mode candidates occur in every sequence, with fractions from 4.27% to
  11.94%;
- the candidate objective improves over the initial local objective on every
  candidate frame;
- no sequence contains a sustained event of three or more consecutive frames;
- no strong-disagreement event was observed under the declared rule.

This is evidence for isolated prior-relative suppression, not evidence for a
production correction. The audit does not authorize estimator-path changes,
parameter tuning, or a claim that the current trajectory APE is caused by the
candidate frames. Any next step must remain an explicitly shadowed experimental
prototype until a new prompt defines an independent gate.
